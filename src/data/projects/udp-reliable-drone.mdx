## Overview

UDP Reliable Drone simulates communication between a ground station and a drone, then adds the reliability primitives that raw UDP deliberately does not provide.

## Engineering focus

- Designed a compact binary protocol with magic bytes and sequence numbers.
- Implemented CRC-8 checksums for payload integrity.
- Added acknowledgments, retry logic, and configurable timeouts.
- Modeled GPS, altitude, and battery telemetry.
- Tested command delivery under simulated 30 percent packet loss.

## Design takeaway

The project makes the delivery and integrity tradeoffs visible: UDP stays lightweight, while the application protocol owns the reliability contract.
